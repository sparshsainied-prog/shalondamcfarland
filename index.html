The Church Inspector

Attorney Tonia Hall is thrust into a world she's been trying her best to avoid: the church.

Pastor Grant hires her to visit a group of struggling churches unannounced, sit among the congregation, and write up what she sees. Each pastor gets her inspection report. Some of what she witnesses only makes her want to keep the church at arm's length.

Then one of the pastors starts digging into her past.

Shalonda

Shalonda McFarland is a Houston author of clean Christian fiction and non-fiction. She is also a songwriter, with one gospel album to her name, You're Glorious Project, sung with Houston singers. And she teaches people how to win with money, God's way.

She's married, with five children. When she couldn't think of a name for the pastor in Sunday Swap, her son Doug Jr came up with Darnell.

  • Business degree from the University of Houston, with a minor in Latin American Studies
  • Played the French horn, but wanted to play the saxophone
  • Her title song You're Glorious was on the Texas Gospel Announcers Guild CD A Message In Our Music
